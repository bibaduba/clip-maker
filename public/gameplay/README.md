# Gameplay backgrounds

Place prepared `.mp4` or `.webm` loops in category subfolders:

- `minecraft/`
- `subway/`
- `cs/`
- `temple-run/`

Subfolders are scanned recursively. The filename becomes the label in the clip
form, for example `subway/run-01.mp4` or `cs/surf-02.webm`. Root-level files
continue to work and are categorized by their filenames. Videos are shown as
real muted previews and are looped under the main clip.
